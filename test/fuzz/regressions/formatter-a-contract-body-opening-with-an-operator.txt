fixed in:  lib/formatter.ml, the `Contract` case of emit_expr_inner

A contract's clauses each take a line, and the body goes on the line
under the last of them. A line that opens with an operator continues the
line above it instead of starting something new, so the body of
`requires q (-1)` came back as `-1` under `requires q` and re-read as
`requires q - 1` -- a clause with no body after it, which does not parse.

The brackets the source had are what said where the clause ended, so the
formatter writes them back around a body that opens with an operator.
