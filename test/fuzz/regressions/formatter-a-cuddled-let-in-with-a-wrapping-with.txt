A `let ... in` cuddled onto `fn -> ` puts its keyword at the end of that
line, while its value and its `in` are laid out from the indent the lambda
was handed -- so every line of the binding sits left of the `let` it
belongs to. The parser reads a line left of the keyword as something new,
which ends the binding early and leaves the `in` with nothing to close.
A `with` body that wrapped was the shape that reached it.

Reduced, and readable, in test_formatter.ml as "a wrapping with inside a
cuddled let-in". Kept here as the input the daily fuzzer reported
