A `%{...}` ends where brace depth returns to zero, and every brace was
counted -- including one inside a `$(...)`, which is shell, where a brace
is an ordinary character. So `"%{$({)}}"` ended at the `}` after the
`)`, the interpolation swallowed a brace the string meant to keep, and
the formatter wrote the literal back one `}` short: source that no longer
lexes.

All three interpolation forms had their own copy of the loop and all
three had the bug, so they share one function now. The three lines here
are one per form.

Nested strings are not handled and do not need to be: a quote or a
backtick inside an interpolation is refused before this matters, so those
inputs never reach the formatter.
