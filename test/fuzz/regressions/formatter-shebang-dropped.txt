The lexer steps over `#!` on line one and emits no token for it, so it
reaches neither the parser nor the pieces the output is assembled from.
The formatter wrote every other line and not that one.

`wand f` writes in place, so formatting a script that runs itself stopped
it running:

  $ wand f script.wand
  $ ./script.wand
  script.wand: line 1: uses: command not found

docs/reference.md documents the form, and nothing was holding it.

The fuzzer would not have found this. Its corpus is the stdlib, the
tests and the examples, and not one of them has a shebang. This fixture
is here so `dune test` holds the line anyway.
