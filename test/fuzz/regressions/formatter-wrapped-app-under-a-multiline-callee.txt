An application that wrapped needs brackets, because it ends where its
first line ends and the lines after it become something else. The guard
for that asked whether the *first* line left a bracket open, which is not
where the risk is once the callee itself spans lines: the block below
opens a bracket on line one and closes it three lines down, and the
argument after that is where the definition quietly ends. The `in` below
was then read as continuing it.

The guard now looks for any line end that closes everything it opened and
still has text after it, rather than only the first.
