`(I)("";1)` is the constructor `I` applied to a block, and it parsed.
The formatter writes an application head without its brackets, so it
came back as `I (""; 1)` -- the same node, and a parse error.

A bracket after a constructor is read as a field list, and the branch
that reads it took an expression and then only `,` or `)`. A `;` can
never be a field list, since a construction names its fields one per
comma, so the bracket is the ordinary one: `Ctor (e1; e2)` and
`Ctor (let x = 1; f x)` are now the constructor applied to that block,
which is what `(Ctor)(e1; e2)` already meant.
