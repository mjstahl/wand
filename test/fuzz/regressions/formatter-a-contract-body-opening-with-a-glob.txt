fixed in:  lib/formatter.ml, the `Contract` case of emit_expr_inner

The sibling of formatter-a-contract-body-opening-with-an-operator. That fix
brackets a contract body that opens with an operator, so the line does not
read as a continuation of the clause above it:

  let x = ensures s **

`**` is a glob literal, and a glob opens with a star. The bracket went
straight onto it and wrote `(**)`, which the lexer reads as an attempt at a
block comment. The body was saved and the file stopped parsing anyway.

`opener` has held the space for this since three earlier findings. The
contract case wrote its bracket by hand instead of going through it. It now
uses `bracket`.
